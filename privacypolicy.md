## 1. What Information Do We Collect?

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you create your account and design your business card, such as:

- Names
    
- Phone numbers
    
- Email addresses
    
- Job titles, departments, and company names
    
- Usernames
    
- Passwords
    
- Profile photos you choose for your card
    
- Business card details (websites, addresses, taglines, biographies, and social media links)
    

We also collect application data, including:

- Card and Wallet Information (the cards you create and the contacts you save inside the app)
    
- Mobile Device Access (Camera for scanning QR codes and capturing profile photos, NFC for sharing and receiving cards)
    
- QR Scan Activity (scan counts for your card)
    

Your information is stored locally on your device and on our servers at https://condor.dz. We do not access your precise location, we do not send you push notifications, and we do not read your phone's contact list — a contact is only created when you choose to save a shared card through your device's Contacts app.

For more details, refer to personal information you disclose to us.

## 2. How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your information to:

- Provide and maintain our Services, including your account and your digital business cards.
    
- Enable you to share your card through QR codes, NFC, and secure links (condor-cards.condor.dz).
    
- Count scans of your card to show you engagement statistics.
    
- Improve user experience.
    
- Comply with legal obligations.
    

For more details, refer to how we process your information.

## 3. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?
